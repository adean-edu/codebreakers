Warm-up

Myth or fact?

Germany’s Enigma machine was unbreakable.

MYTH

Polish mathematicians broke it in 1932, and Britain read it for much of the war.

Codebreakers were found with a newspaper crossword.

FACT

In 1942, people who solved a Daily Telegraph crossword in under 12 minutes were invited to secret tests.

Most of Bletchley Park’s staff were men.

MYTH

About three-quarters of them were women.

A coded WWII pigeon message has never been read.

FACT

It was found in an English chimney in 2012, and nobody has decoded it.
