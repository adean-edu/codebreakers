Every one of these people faced the same question.

How do you use a secret without losing it?

Which story surprised you most? Hold on to it, then play Codebreakers: The Hidden War.

Remember the facts, too. In the game, some classmates will be double agents, and real history is how you catch them.

Every person in this lesson faced the same problem: a secret is only useful if you use it, but using it can give it away. Ask students which story surprised them most, and have a couple share. Then move on to the game, where they will face that question themselves: how do you use a secret without losing it? One more thing to tell them: in the game's Trust or Traitor part, some classmates will secretly be double agents who plant one false fact in their intel reports. Facts from this lesson, like Garbo's 27 imaginary agents or where Operation Mincemeat pointed the Germans, can help them catch a lie, just as real intelligence officers checked reports against what they knew.
