Meet Garbo, one of the strangest spies in history. This photo from MI5's files shows him in disguise. Juan Pujol García was a Spaniard who hated the Nazis. He offered to spy for Britain and was turned down, so he talked the Germans into hiring him instead. From Lisbon, he invented reports about Britain using a guidebook, maps, a railway timetable and newsreels, and the Germans believed him. In 1942 he reached London, where MI5 ran him with his case officer Tomás Harris and a network of 27 imaginary agents, all paid by Germany. The British code-named him Garbo; the Germans called him Arabel. He was awarded both a German Iron Cross and a British MBE. After the war he faked his death and disappeared until a writer tracked him down in 1984. Don't give away what he did in 1944: that is part of the MI5 role in the game.
