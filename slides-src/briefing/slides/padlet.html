Now the Padlet. Sign in with your school account. Step one: post your card in the column for your role, with the card image and the post text from the game. Step two: find cards from the other four roles and type their Signal IDs into the Decoder board in the game, then decide whether to trust their intel reports. Step three: reply to every card you decode. The game writes the reply for you, starting with DECODED and their ID, and you pick a question to ask them. Answer the questions people ask you, too.
