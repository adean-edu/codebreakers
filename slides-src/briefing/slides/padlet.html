Sign in to Padlet with your school account before you post.

Now the Padlet. Sign in with your school account. Step one: post your card in the column for your role, with the card image and the post text from the game. Step two: find cards from the other four roles and type their Signal IDs into the Decoder board in the game, then decide whether to trust their intel reports. Step three: reply to every card you decode. The game writes the reply for you, starting with DECODED, their ID and your proof code, and you pick a question to ask them. The same proof codes are in your submission, so your teacher can search the Padlet and see that every reply was really posted. Answer the questions people ask you, too.
