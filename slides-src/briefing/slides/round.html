How to play

How a round works

Six rounds and a closing epilogue, from 1939 to 1945. Each round has four steps.

1

Briefing

Read the situation, the real history and your private orders.

2

Decrypt

Crack your coded fragment with the cipher dial.

3

Decide

Choose A, B or C. There is no perfect answer.

4

Debrief

See what your choice caused, and what really happened.

Each of the six rounds follows the same four steps. First the briefing: a short newsreel title card, then the situation, a Real History box and an essential question. Second, you decrypt your coded fragment. Third, you make a decision: A, B or C. Read carefully, because each choice has a trade-off. Fourth, the debrief shows what your choice caused, what happened in real history, and whether you made the same call as the real people. After round six, a short epilogue takes you to 1945: the end of the war, and the secret that was kept for almost 30 more years.
