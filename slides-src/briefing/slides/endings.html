Your ending gives your case card its own colour and stamp.

Your gauges at the end of the game decide which of six endings you get. The Quiet Hand is the best: bold, but still hidden. The Burned Source means the enemy found out. The Vault means you kept the secret but never used it. The Lone Operator means you lost the trust of the people who needed you. Each ending gives your case card its own color and stamp, so you can see everyone's endings on the Padlet.
