Step 2

Crack the cipher

  • Every letter was shifted the same number of places.
  • Turn the dial until the green DECODED line reads as English.
  • Stuck? You get three hints, but never the answer.
This is a shift cipher, also called a Caesar cipher. Every letter in your fragment was moved the same number of places in the alphabet. Drag the dial, or use the arrows or slider, until the green decoded line turns into real English words. The signal meter helps you get closer. If you are stuck, the hint button gives up to three clues, but it never gives you the key. The words you decode are your piece of that round's secret message.
