After the game

Five pieces per message

Each round hides a secret message in five pieces, one for each role. You only get yours. Decode classmates from the other four roles to fill every slot.

When all five slots are filled, the round says MESSAGE COMPLETE. Pieces are blacked out here.

Here is the twist. Every round has a secret message, split into five pieces, one for each role. You only ever get your own role's piece. The other four belong to classmates playing the other roles. This is the Decoder board when it's finished: each round has five slots, and they fill in as you decode classmates from those roles. When all five are filled, the round shows MESSAGE COMPLETE and you can read the whole message. You need someone from every other role, so decode widely. The pieces are blacked out on this slide.
