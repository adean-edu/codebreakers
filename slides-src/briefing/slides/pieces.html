After the game

You hold one piece

Each round hides a secret message in five pieces, one for each role. Six rounds, six messages. You can’t read them alone.

The other four pieces unlock when you decode classmates from the other roles.

Here is the twist. Every round has a secret message, split into five pieces, one for each role. You only ever get your own role's piece, pinned to the board. The other four pieces belong to classmates playing the other roles. To read all six messages, you have to work with your class, and that happens on the Padlet.
