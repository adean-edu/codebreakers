Here is how you earn credit. Decode at least one classmate from each of the other four roles. When all six messages are complete, the Decoder board shows this panel with a submission ready to copy, and your Trust or Traitor results unseal here too, so this is where you find out whether your verdicts were right. Type your name, because the game never saves it, and write a sentence or two about what you learned. The submission already includes the six messages, which are blacked out on this slide, your own Signal ID, the Signal IDs of the classmates you decoded, and your verdicts. That is how your teacher checks your work. Copy it and turn it in on edio, in the assignment space. Keep it private: never post it on the Padlet, because anyone could copy it.
