Stuck? Open the Archive. It's a cabinet of declassified files, one file per fact, with a drawer for each role plus the big headline events. Pick a drawer, then click a folder tab. Some details are blacked out, like a real declassified file. Hover over a black bar, or tap it, to see what's underneath. Reading the files is always free. But if you reveal a blacked-out line while an interrogation is open, that verdict counts as "with research", and a perfect verdict scores two points instead of three. So: trust your memory, or pay a point to check.
