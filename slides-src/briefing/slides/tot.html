Trust or traitor

Can you spot a double agent?

Every card on the Padlet carries three intel reports. Some of your classmates will be lying.

Now the new part of the game: Trust or Traitor. Real intelligence services had to decide every day whether to trust their sources. Garbo fooled the Germans for years with reports that were mostly true and carefully false. In this game, every card on the Padlet carries three short intel reports from that student's front. Some of your classmates will secretly be double agents, and one of their reports will be a lie. Your job is to catch them, and, if you choose, to fool them.
