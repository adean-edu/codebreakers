Decode a classmate

  • Type the Signal ID from their Padlet card into the Decoder board.
  • You see their role, their ending and every choice they made.
  • Their pieces of the messages unlock on your board.
  • The game writes your reply, starting with DECODED and their ID.

The message pieces are blacked out on these slides. You’ll see the real words in the game.

Here is what you see when you decode a classmate. Type the Signal ID from their Padlet card into the Decoder board. You get their file: their role, their ending, their score, and the choice they made in every round. Their pieces of each secret message unlock on your board too. I've blacked out the pieces on this slide so I don't give anything away. Below the file, the game writes a reply for you that starts with DECODED and their Signal ID. Pick a question to ask them, copy the reply, and paste it under their card on the Padlet.
