Here's the scoring. You get a point for the right verdict: approving a loyal agent, or denying a double agent. If you catch a double agent, you get another point for naming the right report, and a third for proving the truth. If you used the Archive during that interrogation, a perfect verdict scores two instead of three. As an agent yourself, loyal agents earn a point for every classmate who approves them, and double agents earn a point for every classmate they fool. You won't see whether you were right until you've decoded all six messages. Then your results unseal, right above your submission.
