The real history

Real people, real roles

Bletchley Park Analyst

Alan Turing and Gordon Welchman designed methods and machines to break Enigma. Joan Clarke worked beside Turing on U-boat codes.

Navy Cryptanalyst

Joseph Rochefort led Station HYPO, the Hawaii team behind Midway. Agnes Meyer Driscoll broke earlier Japanese naval codes.

Resistance Courier

Virginia Hall, an American, organized resistance networks in France. Polish couriers smuggled out proof of Germany’s secret rockets.

MI5 Case Officer

Juan Pujol García, code-named Garbo, fooled Germany with 27 imaginary agents. His MI5 case officer was Tomás Harris.

Ultra Liaison Officer

F. W. Winterbotham set up the Special Liaison Units that carried Ultra to commanders and took the paper back.

Every role in the game is based on real people. At Bletchley Park, Alan Turing and Gordon Welchman built the methods and machines that broke Enigma, and Joan Clarke worked alongside Turing on the U-boat codes. Most of Bletchley's staff were women. In Hawaii, Joseph Rochefort's team cracked the code that set up the American victory at Midway, and Agnes Meyer Driscoll had broken earlier Japanese naval codes. Virginia Hall was an American who organized resistance networks in occupied France. Juan Pujol García, code-named Garbo, invented a network of 27 fake spies to fool Germany, working with MI5's Tomás Harris. And Group Captain Winterbotham organized the officers who carried Ultra secrets to generals in the field.
