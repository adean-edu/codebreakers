Trust or traitor

Your secret choice

  • After round 6, choose Loyal agent or Double agent.
  • Loyal: all three of your reports are true.
  • Double: pick one report and choose the lie it will carry.
  • Your Signal ID hides your choice. Nobody knows until the Unmasking.

Once you seal your file, you can’t change your mind. Keep it secret.

After round six, the game asks one last question: are you a loyal agent or a double agent? If you're loyal, all three of your intel reports are true, and you score a point for every classmate who approves you. If you're a double agent, you choose one report to falsify and then choose the lie it will carry, from two false versions. You score a point for every classmate you fool. Your choice is hidden inside your Signal ID, so nobody can tell by looking. Don't tell anyone. I'll reveal who the double agents were at the end, in the Unmasking.
