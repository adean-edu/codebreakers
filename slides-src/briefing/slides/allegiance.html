Can you spot a double agent?

Trust or traitor?

  • Every Padlet card carries three intel reports. Some classmates will be lying.
  • After round 6, choose in secret: Loyal agent (all three reports true) or Double agent.
  • Double: pick one report and choose the lie it will carry.
  • Your Signal ID hides your choice until the Unmasking.

Once you seal your file, you can’t change your mind. Keep it secret.

Now the twist: Trust or Traitor. Real intelligence services had to decide every day whether to trust their sources. Garbo fooled the Germans for years with reports that were mostly true and carefully false. In this game, every card on the Padlet carries three short intel reports from that student's front. After round six, the game asks one last question: are you a loyal agent or a double agent? If you're loyal, all three of your reports are true, and you score a point for every classmate who approves you. If you're a double agent, you choose one report to falsify and then choose the lie it will carry, from two false versions. You score a point for every classmate you fool. Your choice is hidden inside your Signal ID, so nobody can tell by looking. Don't tell anyone. Your teacher will reveal who the double agents were at the end, in the Unmasking.
