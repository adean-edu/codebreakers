The question behind every round

How do you use a secret without losing it?

Every decision you make today is a version of this question.

This is the essential question for the whole game. In World War II, the Allies broke many enemy codes. That gave them a huge advantage, but only as long as the enemy did not find out. If you use a secret too openly, the enemy changes its codes and you go blind. If you never use it, the secret does nothing. Keep this question in mind as you play.
