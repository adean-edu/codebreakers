The rules

Rules of the operation

Do

  • Choose any role you like.
  • Post only your card, once, in your role’s column.
  • Reply to every card you decode.
  • Answer the questions classmates ask you.
  • Keep your allegiance secret.

Don’t

  • Never post decoded messages on the Padlet, in a post or a reply.
  • Never share your six messages with anyone.
  • Don’t post anything except your card and your replies.
  • Never reveal your verdicts or who you think is lying.
A few rules keep the Padlet clean and the assignment fair. Choose any role. Post only your card, once, in your role's column. Reply to every card you decode, and answer the questions people ask you. The most important rule: never post the decoded messages on the Padlet, not in a post and not in a reply. They are your private proof of work. If you post them, everyone else can copy them. And keep Trust or Traitor secret: don't tell anyone whether you're a double agent, and don't share your verdicts. Let everyone make up their own mind.
