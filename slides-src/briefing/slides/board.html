After the game

Fill the Decoder board

Each round’s message has five slots, one per role. Decode classmates from all four other roles, and every slot fills in.

When all five slots in a round are filled, the round says MESSAGE COMPLETE. Pieces are blacked out here.

This is the Decoder board when it's finished. Each round has five slots: Codebreaker, US Navy, Field Agent, Double-Cross and Delivery. Your own role's slot is filled from the start. The others fill in as you decode classmates from those roles. When all five are filled, the round shows MESSAGE COMPLETE and you can read the whole message. You need someone from every other role, so decode widely. The pieces are blacked out on this slide.
