Trust or traitor

The interrogation

1

Approve or deny. Do you trust all three reports?

2

Which report is false? Only if you deny.

3

Prove it. Pick the truth from three choices.

No typing. Verdicts never go on the Padlet, and a filed verdict can’t be changed.

When you decode a classmate, the game opens an interrogation. You see the three intel reports from their card. Step one: approve or deny. Approve means you trust all three. Deny means you think one is a lie. Step two, only if you deny: which report is false? Step three: prove it, by picking what is really true from three choices. Everything is a click; there's no typing. Your verdicts stay inside the game, never on the Padlet, so nobody can see who has already been caught. Think before you press File verdict, because you can't change it.
