Trust or traitor

The interrogation

1

Approve or deny. Do you trust all three reports?

2

Which report is false? Only if you deny.

3

Prove it. Pick the truth from three choices.

Stuck? Open the Archive of declassified files and hover over a black bar to reveal a fact. Doing that during an interrogation makes a perfect verdict worth 2, not 3.

When you decode a classmate, the game opens an interrogation. You see the three intel reports from their card. Step one: approve or deny. Approve means you trust all three. Deny means you think one is a lie. Step two, only if you deny: which report is false? Step three: prove it, by picking what is really true from three choices. Everything is a click; there's no typing. Your verdicts stay inside the game, never on the Padlet, so nobody can see who has already been caught, and a filed verdict can't be changed. Stuck? Open the Archive: a cabinet of declassified files, one per fact, with a drawer for each role. Some details are blacked out; hover over a black bar, or tap it, to see what's underneath. Reading is free, but if you reveal a blacked-out line while an interrogation is open, a perfect verdict scores two points instead of three. Trust your memory, or pay a point to check.
