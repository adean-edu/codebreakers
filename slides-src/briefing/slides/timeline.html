The war at a glance

Jul 1939

Polish codebreakers share their Enigma secrets with Britain and France.

Jun 1942

The US Navy wins the Battle of Midway, sinking four Japanese carriers.

Sep 1939

Germany invades Poland. World War II begins in Europe.

Apr 1943

Admiral Yamamoto’s plane is shot down after his flight plan is decoded.

Jun 1940

France falls to Germany. Britain fights on.

Jun 1944

D-Day: Allied landings in Normandy.

May 1941

The Royal Navy captures a U-boat’s Enigma machine and codebooks.

1945

The war ends. Germany surrenders in May, and Japan in September.

Dec 1941

Japan attacks Pearl Harbor. The United States enters the war.

Here is the big picture the game sits inside. Read down the left column, then the right. The war in Europe began when Germany invaded Poland in September 1939, but just weeks before, Polish codebreakers had handed their Enigma secrets to Britain and France. France fell in 1940. In 1941, the Royal Navy captured Enigma codebooks from a U-boat, and Japan attacked Pearl Harbor. In 1942, American codebreakers helped win at Midway. In 1943, decoded messages led to the shooting down of Admiral Yamamoto, who had planned Pearl Harbor. In June 1944 came D-Day. The war ended in 1945: Germany surrendered in May, and Japan in September. The game covers 1939 to 1944, the years when the secret war was decided.
