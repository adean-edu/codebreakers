How to play

Gauges and endings

Secrecy

Does the enemy still believe its codes are safe?

Impact

Did your intelligence change events?

Trust

Do the people who rely on you still trust you?

Your gauges decide which of six endings you get:

MOST SECRET

The Quiet Hand

IN GOOD ORDER

The Steady Hand

SEALED

The Vault

HIGH RISK

The Gambler

NO CONTACT

The Lone Operator

COMPROMISED

The Burned Source

Three gauges sit at the top of the screen the whole game. Secrecy asks whether the enemy still thinks its codes are safe. Impact asks whether your intelligence actually changed anything. Trust asks whether the people who depend on you still believe you. Being too secret can cost you impact and trust; being too bold can cost you secrecy. At the end, your gauges decide which of six endings you get. The Quiet Hand is the best: bold, but still hidden. The Burned Source means the enemy found out. The Vault means you kept the secret but never used it. The Lone Operator means you lost the trust of the people who needed you. Each ending gives your case card its own colour and stamp, so you can see everyone's endings on the Padlet.
