How to play

Watch your gauges

Every choice moves three gauges. Keeping all three healthy is the challenge.

Secrecy

Does the enemy still believe its codes are safe?

Impact

Did your intelligence change events?

Trust

Do the people who rely on you still trust you?

Your choices and your gauges decide your score, your rank and your ending.

Three gauges sit at the top of the screen the whole game. Secrecy asks whether the enemy still thinks its codes are safe. Impact asks whether your intelligence actually changed anything. Trust asks whether the people who depend on you still believe you. Being too secret can cost you impact and trust. Being too bold can cost you secrecy. The best players balance all three.
