Step 4

What really happened

  • Outcome: what your choice caused.
  • In history: the real event behind the round.
  • The real call: did you choose what the real people chose?

Matching history isn’t the only way to do well. Ask yourself why they chose what they did.

After each decision you get a debrief. The outcome shows what your choice caused and how your gauges moved, with a stamp like Approved or High Risk. The In History box tells you what really happened. The Real Call shows whether you made the same decision as the real people, with a check mark or a cross. Matching history is not the only way to do well, but think about why the real people made the choice they made.
