Gold = your role’s own story. The others are the headline events most of the class shares.

Not everyone plays the same six rounds. Every role shares the headline events, like The Atlantic in round 2 and A Lie for D-Day in round 6. But in some rounds, a role gets its own story instead, shown in gold. For example, in round 4 the Bletchley and Navy players work on Midway, while the Resistance Courier hunts for a leak, the MI5 officer runs Garbo's imaginary spies, and the Ultra Liaison officer deals with convoys to North Africa. When you play your own story, a "Meanwhile" line still tells you what is happening in the headline event. This is why classmates in other roles will have different stories to tell you on the Padlet.
