After the game

Your case card

  • Your name, role, ending and Signal ID
  • Your choice in each of your rounds
  • Post text with one or two questions for classmates
  • Your intel reports stay classified until someone decodes you

Your Signal ID is your proof of play. Classmates type it in to unlock your message pieces and read your intel.

When you finish all six rounds, you get a case card. It shows your name, your role, your ending and your score, plus your Signal ID. The Signal ID is a code that holds all of your choices. When a classmate types your Signal ID into their game, they see what you chose in every round and they unlock your pieces of the messages. You can download or copy the card image, and the game also gives you post text to copy. The card shows the title of each of your rounds beside the choice you made. In the post text you pick one or two questions for your classmates. Your three intel reports are classified: they show as black bars on the Padlet, and a classmate only sees them after decoding your Signal ID. That's where Trust or Traitor happens.
