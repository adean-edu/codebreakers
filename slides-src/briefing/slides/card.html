After the game

Your case card

  • Your name, role and ending
  • Your Signal ID, like BP-ACCBCC-C-GY
  • A question for your classmates
  • In the post text: three intel reports from your front

Your Signal ID is your proof of play. Classmates type it in to read your choices and unlock your pieces.

When you finish all six rounds, you get a case card. It shows your name, your role, your ending and your score, plus your Signal ID. The Signal ID is a code that holds all of your choices. When a classmate types your Signal ID into their game, they see what you chose in every round and they unlock your pieces of the messages. You can download or copy the card image, and the game also gives you post text to copy. The post text carries three intel reports from your front, and that's where Trust or Traitor happens.
