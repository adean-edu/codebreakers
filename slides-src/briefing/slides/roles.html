How to play

Choose your role

Bletchley Park Analyst

Breaks German ciphers in England.

Navy Cryptanalyst

Reads Japan’s naval codes for the US Navy.

Resistance Courier

Carries messages and proof across occupied Europe.

MI5 Case Officer

Runs double agents who feed lies to Germany.

Ultra Liaison Officer

Carries secret intelligence to generals.

Pick any role. Each one holds a different piece of every secret message, so the class needs all five.

There are five roles, and you choose your own. Bletchley Park Analysts break German ciphers. Navy Cryptanalysts work on Japan's codes. Resistance Couriers carry messages through occupied Europe. MI5 Case Officers run double agents. Ultra Liaison Officers deliver secret intelligence to generals without saying where it came from. Some rounds are your role's own story, so two classmates in different roles will see different events. Make sure every role is played by someone in the class, or nobody can finish the messages.
